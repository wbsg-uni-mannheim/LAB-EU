# Legal review

Material for the review of the error classes by a legal expert.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `pruefung-fehlerklassen.pdf` | The review form in German, test version with 13 examples: one per K1 level and per further class, and four boundary cases (about 30 to 45 minutes). Each example is a short box: the criterion and the year the case was published, where needed a quotation from the case facts or the instructions to candidates as background, with a short note, what the criterion expects, what the answer does, and the assigned class with one or two sentences on this answer. Where the two annotators assigned different classes, the box also names the class that was not chosen and the argument for it. The reviewer marks whether the class fits the definitions, whether a corrector would object by examination standards and, if so, how much the error weighs (1 to 3). The quotations from the answer, the model solution, the criterion in full, a short summary of the case and, where needed, excerpts from the case facts and a note on the assignment are in an appendix. |
| `error-classes-overview.pdf` | One-page overview of the classes. |
| `error-classes.pdf` | The full definitions and boundaries. |
| `volltexte/` | For reference only, one PDF per example: the criterion, the task instruction, the full case facts (for ÖR 15 also the statute extracts given with the task), the assessed answer with the line numbers the form cites as "Z.", and the full model solution. The wording is taken unchanged from the files of the study, only the Markdown formatting is rendered, and each PDF is checked against its source texts. Each PDF names the source and licence of the case (CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0). |

The form has no links into the repository. Each box gives the page of its evidence in the appendix and the name of its full-text file.

**Selection.** Drawn at random with a fixed seed from the final assignments, excluding consequential errors (Folgefehler) and ÖR 12, whose task question named the wrong plaintiff. The form has 13 examples: one per K1 level and per further class, and one disputed case each at the boundaries K1a/K1b, K1b/K1c, K2/K3 and K1/OK, from different cases where possible. Once the format has been tested with a legal expert, it will be extended to up to five examples per class.

The list of rubric flags may be added later.
