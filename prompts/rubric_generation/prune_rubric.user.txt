Prune the candidate criteria into a final rubric.

Rules:
- Choose the number of final criteria based on this case. There is no fixed target count.
- Prefer the smallest set that still covers all material requirements of a high-quality answer.
- Keep only material checks a supervising lawyer would require before accepting the answer.
- Remove duplicates and near-duplicates.
- Merge overlapping candidates only when the merged criterion remains clearly Boolean.
- Split or reject compound candidates that cannot be judged as a single pass/fail check.
- Prefer criteria about outcome-changing legal issues, correct legal basis, rule content, facts, application/subsumption, conclusion, source grounding, and required legal form.
- Do not overfit to the human solution's exact wording or order.
- The final criteria must remain pass/fail and must be in the same language as the case.
- Preserve explicit Boolean labels in the case language, such as "ERFÜLLT" / "NICHT ERFÜLLT" for German.
- Keep each final criterion concise enough for a judge prompt.
- Record rejected candidate IDs with a short reason.
