## Content verdict

- Accept equivalent legal wording and a valid alternative structure unless the criterion

  necessarily and unambiguously from what the answer itself says on the same question.

  was made. A component is implied only when the answer's own statements on this question contain its substance (the decisive fact, legal step, or conclusion) in other words. It is not implied when the answer never addresses a step the criterion requires, such as a prerequisite, the exclusion of an alternative, or a restrictive interpretation, even if the answer's result is consistent with that step.
