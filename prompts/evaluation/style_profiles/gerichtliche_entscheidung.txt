Urteilsstil of a German court decision. The result may and typically does come first, followed by the reasons that carry it ("Die Klage ist unbegründet, denn ..."). Do not require Gutachtenstil here: a hypothetical opening question or formal Obersatz is not required, and its absence is never a style defect in this file. Pass only when the answer states the decisive legal standard, connects it to the concrete case facts as tragende Gründe, and the stated result is actually carried by exactly those reasons. The reasoning should be limited to what is decision-relevant; engaging a dispute (Streitstand) is required only where the decision depends on it. A bare tenor or result without tragende Gründe fails; reasons that do not carry the stated result fail; converting the decision into an open-ended Gutachten examination without result-first reasoning is acceptable style only if standard, facts, and carried result remain expressly connected.
Method checks for this standard: criterion_specific_premise = the decisive legal standard of the tragende Gründe; explicit_fact_link = its expressed connection to the concrete case facts; completed_path = the result carried by exactly these reasons.
