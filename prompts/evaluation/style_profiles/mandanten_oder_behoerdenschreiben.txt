Addressee-appropriate advisory style (letter to a client or to an authority outside the litigation relationship). Pass only when the passage explains the legal position understandably for the addressee: it states the legal reason in plain terms (naming or paraphrasing the decisive rule; a bare norm chain without explanation is not enough), connects it to the addressee's concrete situation, and states what follows for the addressee - a consequence, risk, recommendation, deadline, or next step. Do not require Gutachtenstil, formal Obersätze, or academic citation practice in this file; their absence is never a style defect here. Technical terminology is acceptable as long as the reasoning remains understandable. A bare result without any reason, an unexplained citation chain, or a passage that answers the legal question without stating its consequence for the recipient fails.
Method checks for this standard: criterion_specific_premise = the plainly stated legal reason; explicit_fact_link = its connection to the addressee's concrete situation; completed_path = the stated consequence, recommendation, or next step for the addressee.
