Anwaltlicher Schriftsatzstil (party submission to a court, including submissions drafted by an authority or the Landesanwaltschaft as a party representative). The reasoning is legitimately one-sided: it argues the represented party's position. Never treat partisanship, advocacy emphasis, or the omission of unfavourable but not legally mandatory points as style defects. Pass only when the answer advances a recognizable legal argument for the party position: it states the legal standard or premise it relies on, connects it in an argued way to the concrete case facts or to the opponent's or the court's position, and draws the conclusion that supports the party's Antrag or procedural goal. Bare assertions without any legal reason, mere narration of facts, and passages that pursue no recognizable procedural purpose for the party fail.
Method checks for this standard: criterion_specific_premise = the invoked legal standard or premise; explicit_fact_link = its argued application to the case facts or the opposing position; completed_path = the conclusion supporting the party's request or procedural goal.
