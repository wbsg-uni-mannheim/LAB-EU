Functional Gutachtenstil (internal legal examination). Pass only when the answer itself connects (1) a criterion-specific legal standard or decisive premise (Obersatz or an equivalent stated standard), (2) the expressed application of that standard to concrete facts or competing arguments (Subsumtion), and (3) a conclusion that completes this exact line of examination (Ergebnis). The exact order and wording may vary; a rigid four-sentence template is not required. A bare conclusion, a definition without application, a list of facts without legal evaluation, or an examination path that is opened but not completed fails.
Method checks for this standard: criterion_specific_premise = the stated standard or decisive premise; explicit_fact_link = the expressed subsumption of concrete facts under it; completed_path = the conclusion of exactly this examination.
